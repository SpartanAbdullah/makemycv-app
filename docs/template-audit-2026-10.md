# Template audit — 2026-10-24

Scope: all ten CV templates (live render in `lib/templates/*.tsx`, PDF layout in `components/pdf/CVDocument.tsx`, registry copy and badges, August preview PNGs), plus market research on which template designs UAE job seekers actually use, to decide which templates to add next.

Method: one independent reviewer per template (PNG, live source, PDF source, registry entry), one skeptic per high-severity claim told to refute it (16 verdicts: 0 refuted, 16 upheld — 7 kept at high, the rest downgraded to medium), three research lenses (UAE recruiters/portals, big-builder best sellers, UAE-focused competitors), one synthesis. Run via the Workflow tool, 30 agents. Raw per-agent output: session workflow `wf_e61c9e02-1ba`.

## Defects, prioritised (56)

"Verified" = a skeptic agent tried to refute the claim against the code and failed.

### 1. [high, verified] executive, exec-split, corp-sidebar, onyx, sandstone

Every rgba() border colour in CVDocument.tsx renders as solid neon green in the downloaded PDF: @react-pdf/stylesheet turns rgba into 8-digit hex (#FFFFFF26) and pdfkit _normalizeColor misparses it to [0,255,38]. Confirmed at PDF-operator level. Hits the Executive rule under the headline (:268) on every download with a headline, and the photo ring on every photo download in Executive (:826), Exec Split (:1909), Corporate (:2307), Onyx bandBorder (:2743) and Sandstone (:2994).

**Fix:** Replace every rgba border/colour prop in CVDocument.tsx with an opaque hex computed by mix(theme.onAccent, theme.accent, t) from lib/utils/color.ts (t 0.85 for 15% white, 0.80 for 20%, 0.75 for 25%); use the same computed hex in the live templates so preview and download match; add a grep guard in the pdf-smoke script that fails on 'rgba(' in CVDocument.tsx border/color props (fills are safe but ban them too for simplicity).

### 2. [high, verified] modern

The Modern PDF is a different document from the preview: live is a 2fr/1fr page with Education and Skills in a 200px right aside and main order Profile > Experience > Languages > Certifications > Projects; ModernPDFLayout (CVDocument.tsx:1481-1704) is a single-column stack ordered Summary > Experience > Education > Skills > Languages|Certifications > Projects with the first heading renamed 'Summary'. The router comment at :3267 acknowledges Modern is two-column while placing it in SINGLE_COLUMN.

**Fix:** Rewrite ModernPDFLayout as a flexDirection 'row' body (main flex 2, aside ~150pt flexShrink 0, gap 24pt) mirroring the live column assignment and order, heading 'Profile'; or, if the single-column PDF is declared the truth, change modern.tsx to match. One layout, both renderers.

### 3. [high, verified] sandstone

PDF language names are hardcoded #292524 (CVDocument.tsx:3138) while live uses theme.onAccent (sandstone.tsx:269). With the store's default #1e5b54 accent, and 10 of 11 presets, onAccent is white, so language names print at ~1.9:1 on the band (Graphite 1.07:1) and are effectively invisible in the download while the preview shows them white; the ' — Native' suffix stays white, leaving a dangling level.

**Fix:** Change CVDocument.tsx:3138 to color: onAccent (SIDE_TEXT), matching sandstone.tsx:269 and the Onyx PDF at :2874.

### 4. [high, verified] executive

On a two-page Executive PDF the sidebar band on page 2+ starts 27pt below the top edge, leaving a white notch: execSidebar (CVDocument.tsx:243-256) bleeds via negative margins, and react-pdf's splitNode rebuilds the continuation with marginTop 0. Pixel-verified on a 9-role render; an 18-year executive CV routinely runs to two pages.

**Fix:** Draw the band separately: a fixed View with position 'absolute', left 0, top 0, bottom 0, width 150, backgroundColor theme.accent rendered first on the Page; make execSidebar transparent and remove its negative margins. Apply the same pattern to the other bleed sidebars when their margin handling is reworked.

### 13. [medium, verified] sandstone (registry-wide)

## Cross-cutting themes

- One shared primitive, nine templates: the PDF layouts reuse Classic's BASE_STYLES and shared blocks (s.sectionHeading, the 'Role | Company' experience row, EducationEntry, type sizes) instead of mirroring each live template, which is the root of the heading-style, entry-structure, education-anatomy and type-size parity gaps in five to nine templates at once. Fix at the primitive level with variant props (or per-layout blocks) and add a live-vs-PDF parity smoke (page count per preview persona, text extraction of key lines) so the next template cannot regress silently.
- rgba() is unsafe in react-pdf: @react-pdf/stylesheet emits 8-digit hex that pdfkit misparses, so every translucent border in CVDocument.tsx strokes neon green. Rule: colours handed to react-pdf must be opaque hex computed with mix() from lib/utils/color.ts, mirrored live, and guarded by a grep in the pdf-smoke script.
- Hardcoded colours in the sidebar PDFs defeat the contrast-safe theme: Corporate #CBD5E1 contact values, Sandstone #292524 language names, Corporate's live white-alpha labels, Corporate's navy name rule. The live templates mostly route through onAccent/onAccentMuted/accentText; the PDFs must too, and a smoke assertion should check every sidebar text colour at >=3:1 against theme.accent for all presets (Sand #C9A96A is the stress case).
- Density scales are applied unevenly: marginScale moves all four edges live but only the sidebar gutter in the bleed PDFs and nothing at all in Exec Split; spaceScale scales between-section gaps live but entry gaps and twoCol in the PDF, while sidebar labels are scaled live and not in the PDF; first sections carry the between-section margin in four PDF layouts. Restructure bleed pages (Page padding 0, main column owns scaled padding, absolute band) and trim SECTION_GAP_KEYS so theme.ts's definitions hold in both renderers.
- Chips flatten to text in the PDF: skills chips (ATS Clean, Exec Split, Corporate, Modern) and UAE essentials chips (Modern, Professional, Professional Photo) become joined strings, with one hardcoded indigo separator. Build a single shared PDF chip primitive (Onyx already has one) with theme colours before adding any template that uses chips, or decide per template that the text line is the truth and change the preview to match.
- Content is silently dropped or mis-gated in the download: project links in the three 2026-10 layouts, the attested tick glyph (no embedded font has U+2713), a bare 'Attested' without a body, placeholder words 'Role'/'Degree'/'Project', inert contact links in Professional, whitespace-only bullets and summaries treated differently. The PDF is the submitted artefact; every live-only guard must exist in the PDF and vice versa.
- Badge and copy honesty: Onyx and Sandstone (two-column) lack the Design-led badge the registry rule requires; Professional Photo lacks the ATS-Friendly badge its identical single-column sibling carries; Executive and Corporate share one description; ATS Clean uses an unverifiable superlative; thumbnails do not match (Classic, Modern) and the in-app gallery never renders the Thumbnail field at all. The product's ATS promise depends on these signals being accurate.
- Accent reach is inconsistent and the store seed overrides template identity: dead on Classic, header-rule-only on ATS Clean, PDF-only on Modern, sidebar-only in the Executive PDF; CustomizePanel shows the swatches for every template; and the #1e5b54 seed plus templateId-only switching means Sandstone is never beige and Onyx/Executive signature fallbacks never apply. Introduce TemplateDefinition.defaultAccent with reset-on-switch and gate the Colours group per template.
- Empty-shell filtering stops at Experience/Education/Projects: Languages and Certifications have no meaningful* helpers, so blank cards print ghost headings and ' — Conversational' lines everywhere, including the DOCX path.
- UAE essentials, the product differentiator, are the least consistently rendered element: inline chips in the single-column templates, a 'Personal' block in Executive/Corporate, 'Details' last in the Onyx band, a label/value panel in Sandstone that prints 'DL', flattened text in three PDFs, and colour emoji beside them on Classic/Executive. The research is unanimous that visa status and availability are the most-screened lines; they should get consistent, labelled, text-first treatment and the same weight in both renderers.
- Sidebar layouts under-use the page and demote the summary: Executive, Onyx and Exec Split leave 40-47% of a typical page blank, Exec Split and Onyx push the summary into the narrow column, and photo rings/bleeds carry the react-pdf page-break and alpha gotchas. New templates should prefer single-column bodies and in-flow photos; sidebar variants already exist four times over.
- Preview assets and invariants are drifting: three preview personas duplicate the field inside the degree (visible in six shipped PNGs), seven template roots lack .cv-print despite the CLAUDE.md invariant, the px->pt comment is stale, and router comments describe behaviour the code does not have. Recapture previews and correct the docs in the same pass as the fixes.

## Market research

### Lens: What UAE/GCC recruiters, HR teams and the ATS portals of large UAE employers actually prefer in a CV layout in 2025-26 (Bayt, GulfTalent, NaukriGulf guidance; live portal detection; parsing tests), cross-checked against what job seekers in the region search for and download, and against the ten templates already in makemycv-app (lib/templates/*.tsx).

- Gulf CV convention is 'personal details first'. Bayt's 2026 UAE guide orders the CV: personal details (name, contact, location, nationality, visa status, optional DOB) -> summary -> experience -> education -> skills -> certifications -> languages, 1-2 pages, and calls a professional headshot 'expected rather than optional' (https://www.bayt.com/en/blog/32538/cv-format-for-uae-jobs-the-complete-2026-guide/). GulfTalent's Dubai guide: 'Your passport nationality needs to be mentioned on your CV under your personal details'; 'Most companies in Dubai prefer to see your photo on the CV. For multinationals and for more senior roles, this tends to be less common'; '1-2 page CV is the right length to submit, even if you have decades of experience' (https://www.gulftalent.com/resources/dubai-jobs-guide/building-your-profile). NaukriGulf: 'Dubai recruiters also look out for a photograph of the candidate, so do include a recent photo' (https://www.naukrigulf.com/career-tips/what-recruiters-check-in-your-cv). Marital status and religion are now 'leave out unless asked'; Emirates ID and passport numbers should never appear (https://atsverification.com/blog/dubai-uae-resume-format-2026/, https://www.labeeb.ae/abu-dhabi-government-cv-guide).
- Nationality, visa status and availability are filter fields, not decoration. GulfTalent lets recruiters filter its CV database by '20 different criteria - including education, experience, industry, location, nationality, age and salary expectation' and says 'Many employers strongly prefer candidates who are available immediately and do not have to ... go through the process of visa change' (https://www.gulftalent.com/resources/dubai-jobs-guide/building-your-profile). NaukriGulf's recruiter guide filters by 'age, visa status, languages' (https://www.naukrigulf.com/ni/nidownload/Naukri_gulf_Communication/Recruiter_Communication/Recruiters_quick_guide_NG.pdf). Emirates NBD's own careers form asks for Nationality (dropdown), residence emirate and a High School / University Graduate / Experienced track (https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/careers/careers-form). So these fields belong as plain, labelled text near the top of every template, never in a sidebar or image.
- Which ATS the big UAE employers actually run (checked on their live careers pages, 2026-10-24): Emaar -> Oracle HCM/Recruiting Cloud (emhm.fa.em2.oraclecloud.com, https://www.emaar.com/careers/); DP World -> Oracle Recruiting Cloud (ehpv.fa.em2.oraclecloud.com, https://www.dpworld.com/en/careers/vacancies/emirati-talent-future-opportunities-orc-req-12604); First Abu Dhabi Bank -> Oracle Recruiting Cloud (ehjd.fa.em2.oraclecloud.com/.../fabCareers, https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/about-fab/careers); Etisalat UAE (e&) -> Oracle HCM (iaayey.fa.ocs.oraclecloud26.com, https://careers.etisalat.ae/en/index.jsp); Emirates Group -> Avature (emiratesjobs.avature.net, https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/search-and-apply/); Etihad -> SmartRecruiters (https://careers.etihad.com/); Emirates NBD -> custom form. wmtips puts Oracle Cloud HCM at 46.8% of HR software detected on UAE websites, SAP SuccessFactors 12.9%, and notes Taleo is 'more popular in India, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar' than Workday (https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/hr-management/country/ae/). The blog claims that ADNOC/Emirates run SuccessFactors could not be verified (ADNOC careers pages 404/DNS-fail); treat vendor lists in CV blogs as unverified. Practical consequence: Oracle Recruiting Cloud's resume parser is what most MakeMyCV users will hit, and it is line-based - single-column, in-flow text wins.
- Single-column is the portal default; two-column is for direct email/WhatsApp. The one controlled test found a two-column sidebar the only 'critical' breaker (85/B vs 100/A for single column; skills grid and em-dashes did not break), but it was one resume through a pdf.js-style parser, not a real ATS (https://atsverification.com/blog/ats-parsing-benchmark-2026/). Labeeb and ATS Verification both say contact details must sit in the body, not a page header, and 'no text boxes, no tables for the body' (https://www.labeeb.ae/abu-dhabi-government-cv-guide, https://atsverification.com/blog/dubai-uae-resume-format-2026/). Download behaviour matches: Kickresume's 2025 analysis of ~420K downloads found 'nearly 62% of downloaded resumes used templates classified as ATS-friendly' and the most-downloaded designs 'shared single-column layouts, clear section hierarchy, restrained use of color' (https://www.kickresume.com/en/press/resume-statistics-structure/); Resume.io's single-column 'Classic/London' shows '42,000,000+ users' vs 12M for the two-column 'Stockholm' (https://resume.io/resume-templates), while Zety's most-popular is the two-column 'Cascade' (https://zety.com/lp/resume-templates). MakeMyCV already has six two-column designs and four single-column ones; the gap is in single-column variants with Gulf-specific structure.
- Page count: 1-2 pages is the stated norm (Bayt 2026, GulfTalent, Agile Consultants, Mahad Manpower); 'three for senior technical or academic profiles' (https://www.labeeb.ae/abu-dhabi-government-cv-guide); 'never exceed beyond two, maximum of three pages' (https://www.resumeble.com/career-advice/what-is-the-correct-uae-cv-format). Fresh graduates: one page. Bayt's poll of 9,144 respondents across 18 countries found 79.5% want a fresh-graduate CV of one page max, 56.5% rate education the most important section, 89% want a career objective, 62.6% want grades shown, and 75.2% think graduates should include a photo (https://kuwaittimes.com/bayt-com-provides-insights-perfect-cv-look-like-fresh-graduate).
- Photo is sector-dependent, and both answers are 'right'. Bayt 2025: photo 'expected - but there are rules'; include for appearance-driven roles (retail, aviation, hospitality), skip for technical/corporate; a photo 'could disrupt the formatting or lead to technical issues during parsing' (https://www.bayt.com/en/blog/32303/should-i-put-my-photo-on-my-cv-in-the-gcc/). Arabian Business experts: more common for client-facing roles, 'less necessary for senior positions' (https://www.arabianbusiness.com/jobs/uae-jobs-should-jobseekers-put-their-photograph-on-a-cv-experts-weigh-in). VisualCV: expected by local/regional employers, optional at multinationals and free-zone firms (https://www.visualcv.com/international/dubai-resume/). Airline cabin-crew applications ask for a 3.5x4.5 cm passport headshot plus a full-length photo as separate uploads (https://cabincrew24.com/emirates-cabin-crew-photo-requirements/). Design rule that follows: the photo must be an in-flow <img> (never a CSS background or sidebar), passport-proportioned rather than round for the Gulf-traditional templates, and the existing showPhoto toggle must stay one click away.
- Emiratisation and government portals are a distinct track. Labeeb's guides (the only detailed source; a Dubai CV consultancy, so one perspective): single column, standard headings, no tables/text boxes; 'Side-by-side Arabic and English columns are the most common bilingual CV failure' - produce a separate single-column Arabic RTL PDF instead; Nafis recruiters 'search the structured profile fields rather than the uploaded PDF' (UAE PASS login, up to 3 CVs); TAMM accepts PDF only, 2MB; FAHR prefers Arabic; Dubai Careers is English-default, 5MB PDF/DOCX (https://labeeb.ae/bilingual-english-arabic-cvs-for-emiratisation-applications-best-practices/, https://www.labeeb.ae/abu-dhabi-government-cv-guide). Emirates Group, Etihad and Emirates NBD all run dedicated 'UAE Nationals' application tracks (https://careers.smartrecruiters.com/EtihadAirways5/uae-nationals). None of the ten existing templates has an Arabic name line, RTL field support or Emirati-specific fields.
- Data-model check against lib/types/cv.ts: CvPersonal already carries nationality, dateOfBirth, drivingLicense, visaStatus, availability, location, linkedin, photo/showPhoto, and CvData has languages. The ten existing templates surface the UAE fields only as inline chips via lib/utils/essentials.ts (Visa / Available / DL / Nationality) plus a 'DOB:' item in the contact line; the only labelled 'Personal Details' block is Sandstone's, inside a two-column beige sidebar. There is no maritalStatus field (keep it that way - sources now say omit unless asked), no Arabic-name field, and no education-first ordering option (section-order work was deferred per memory notes). Candidates 1, 2, 3 and 5 below need no schema change; candidate 4 needs two optional fields.

### 1. Gulf Standard

The shape every 'UAE CV format' guide and free-template site describes; converged on independently by the recruiter lens and the UAE-competitor lens._

**Layout spec**

**Gap it fills**

**Build notes**

Density: wire all four scales from day one (page padding * marginScale in both renderers, --fs/--lh/--sp live, buildStyles PDF), first section marginTop 0, spaceScale only on heading marginTop. Do not inherit the audit debt: write the education block with the de-dup rule instead of the shared EducationEntry (or use the variant props once they exist), no rgba colours, no emoji, no chips in the PDF, own heading style rather than s.sectionHeading, project links rendered in both paths. DOCX: docxExport.ts needs the same section order and a two-column details block built from paragraphs or a borderless table (ATS-safe when borderless and 2-col). Preview: new expat persona whose field is not inside the degree; thumbnail must show the details grid and square photo. Tests: pdf-smoke plus a pdf.js text-extraction check that 'Visa status' label and value share one line and that page count matches the live sheet.

It is distinct from all ten (Exec Split is the only band and it sits on a two-column body; the other photo templates use sidebars or a white centred header), it avoids the sidebar failure modes this audit catalogued, and its body is linearly parseable, so it can wear ATS-Friendly if the DOCX export keeps the band as a shaded paragraph, or Design-led otherwise; either way the pair gives the picker one traditional and one modern photo option plus an unambiguous single-column anchor. Chronicle is the immediate third: it is the most distinct candidate and the first design-forward template that can honestly be badged ATS-Friendly, but its UAE-specific evidence is thinner than Banner's. Harvard is fourth, or a Classic retune, because of near-neighbour risk. Two candidates with strong evidence were parked rather than shortlisted: the Emirati/Government Portal CV (two lenses) needs new optional fields (arabicName, nationalService, familyBook, UAE-national chip handling) and should be built as a photo-off Gulf Standard variant once the schema lands; Technical Projects and Europass Gulf are section-order or field toggles on Gulf Standard, and the Enhancv-style Achiever is a Modern refresh, not a new template. Build-order caveat: the audit shows the PDF debt is in shared primitives (rgba borders, EducationEntry, heading/experience blocks, chips, marginScale on bleed pages), so the six verified highs and the primitive-level mediums should land in the same sprint as the new templates, and both new templates must be written against the corrected primitives, not the current ones, or they inherit every parity gap listed above. Modern (score 2) is the worst offender and should be fixed or re-based before the gallery grows.
